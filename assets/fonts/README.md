Bundled faces are unchanged copies of the shared consumer font set used by Knot and Cleromancy. IBM Plex Mono and Source Serif 4 retain their family names, weights, styles and accompanying OFL.txt notices. Metadata registration does not rename these faces or certify complete glyph coverage.

Sources: https://github.com/IBM/plex and https://github.com/adobe-fonts/source-serif .

Bravura, in `bravura/`, supplies unmodified SMuFL musical glyph outlines for
native chart/tab/score painting. It is used by the notation leaf rather than
as a body-text appearance choice. Its OFL and upstream attribution accompany
the font.
